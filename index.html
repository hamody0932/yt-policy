N8N8 Application

Welcome to N8N8, an automated workflow tool designed to manage and upload personal video content to YouTube using the official YouTube Data API.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the N8N8 application handles your data:

  • Data Access: N8N8 accesses your YouTube account strictly to perform authorized video uploads and metadata management on your behalf.
  • Data Storage: We do not store, log, or sell any personal data, account credentials, or uploaded media to external servers.
  • Third Parties: No information accessed via Google APIs is shared with any third parties.
